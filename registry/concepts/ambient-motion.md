# Ambient motion

Ambient (decorative) motion moves without input. It sets a mood, but it competes for attention and never stops on its own.

Rules: keep it slow, use it once per page, and give it an off switch. Moving content that lasts more than five seconds needs a way to pause it (WCAG 2.2.2), and it must respect [[reduced-motion]].

[[orbit-animation]] is ambient motion. It becomes an interaction once it responds to the visitor, for example by pausing on hover.
