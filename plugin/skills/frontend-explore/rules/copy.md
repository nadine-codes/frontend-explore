# Copy tone

- Plain, short sentences. Say what the thing does, then stop.
- Concrete beats clever: "Tilts toward your pointer" over "Elevate your experience".
- Banned phrases: "Unlock", "Seamless", "Elevate", "Supercharge", "Revolutionize", "In today's fast-paced world".
- No em dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- Buttons say what happens: "Start exploring", not "Submit" or "Click here".
- Keep a light personality if the brand has one, but never at the cost of clarity.
