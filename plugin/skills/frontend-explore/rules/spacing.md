# Spacing

- Pick a base unit (4px or 8px) and use multiples. Do not invent one-off values.
- Vary the rhythm. Related things sit close (8 to 16px); separate sections get real air (48 to 96px). Equal gaps everywhere look generated.
- Cap reading width (about 640 to 760px) even on wide pages. Let visuals use the full width.
- Keep side gutters at least 16px on phones.
- Align to a few strong edges. One clear left edge beats many centered blocks.
